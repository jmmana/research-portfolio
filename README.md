# Juan Manuel Castillo Pinto

## Research portfolio

I am an Informatics Engineer and AI / UiPath Solution Architect based in Colombia, currently completing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at Universidad de La Salle, Bogotá. My work connects enterprise data systems, intelligent automation and applied AI research.

**Research direction:** building trustworthy AI systems that interact with structured data and produce outputs that can be evaluated, explained and audited.

I am preparing for full-time doctoral study beginning in Fall 2027, with an interest in research assistantship opportunities in data science and artificial intelligence.
