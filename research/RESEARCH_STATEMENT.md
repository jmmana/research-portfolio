# Research statement

Juan Manuel Castillo Pinto · November 2026

## Direction

I want to investigate how AI systems can work reliably with structured, consequential data. My starting point is a background in enterprise databases, software integration and intelligent automation, together with graduate research in artificial intelligence. The common thread is the need to connect a prediction or generated answer with the constraints and evidence that make it usable.

## AI and structured data

Natural-language interfaces can make enterprise information easier to query, but a syntactically valid query can still be semantically wrong or outside the user's authority. I am interested in systems that combine schema grounding, policy enforcement, uncertainty handling and an auditable record of how an answer was produced.

Potential experiments would compare unconstrained generation with schema-aware validation, execution controls and abstention. Evaluation should distinguish answer correctness, permission compliance, harmful-query rejection, latency and failure recovery. Synthetic or explicitly shareable database schemas can support reproducibility without exposing client information.

These are proposed research questions. They are not claims of completed comparative experiments.

## Medical computer vision

My master's project, BoneAgeTW2, explores skeletal maturation assessment from pediatric hand radiographs using the Tanner–Whitehouse 2 framework. The documented architecture connects YOLOv8 localization with EfficientNet-B3 stage classification and structured reporting.

The research questions I want to strengthen include learning with limited expert annotations, evaluating bone-level errors, measuring calibration and testing generalization across populations and acquisition settings. A research report should clearly separate expert labels from pseudo-labels and learned predictions from anatomical or age-conditioned priors.

## Research practice

I value explicit dataset splits, reproducible execution, honest baselines and a clear account of failure modes. In particular, a demonstration should not substitute for a measured evaluation, and a source dataset's size should not be confused with the number of cases actually annotated or tested.

## Doctoral objective

I am preparing for doctoral study in data science and artificial intelligence beginning in Fall 2027. I hope to contribute my experience with databases and production software to a research group working on trustworthy AI, data systems or medical machine learning. A specific dissertation topic should develop through a well-defined research problem and collaboration with an advisor.
